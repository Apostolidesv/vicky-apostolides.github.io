Projects

Alaska Braidplains NDVI

As a research assistant working under Dr. William Armstrong, I investigate how proglacial lakes influence vegetation establishment and ecological succession across Alaskan braidplains. My work utilizes Landsat imagery, Python, and GIS to analyze multidecadal changes in NDVI and channel stability in glacier-fed environments.

Tools: GIS, Python, Landsat, NDVI, remote sensing

Stream Mapping in Central Kentucky Streams (REU)

As an NSF REU researcher working under Dr. Michael Sama, I use unmanned aerial systems (UAS), photogrammetry, and machine learning to assess vegetation health along Central Kentucky stream systems. My work involves autonomous drone flight planning, orthomosaic generation in Pix4D, Random Forest classification, and SAM2 segmentation to quantify riparian condition and evaluate the impacts of flooding and human disturbance.

Tools: UAS, drone imagery, Pix4D, ArcGIS Pro, stream mapping

Flooding in Kentucky Catchments (KGS)

As a Potter Intern with the Kentucky Geological Survey under Dr. Meredith Swallom, I analyze hydrologic and meteorological datasets to better understand flooding in central Kentucky. My work combines Python, ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, and TUFLOW to model flood behavior, assess precipitation trends, and support hazard mitigation efforts.

Tools: GIS, flood data, precipitation analysis, TUFLOW, Notepad++, Kentucky Geological Survey

E. coli in Kentucky Rivershed

As a student researcher in the Washburn CHAOS Lab, I analyzed citizen science data collected through Watershed Watch in Kentucky, a statewide environmental monitoring program. Using QGIS and statistical analyses, I investigated spatial and temporal patterns of E. coli concentrations, rainfall, and turbidity across the Kentucky River Basin. My work included data cleaning, geospatial visualization, and the development of monthly map series to better understand environmental drivers of microbial contamination and support watershed management efforts.

Tools: QGIS, water quality data, spatial analysis, E. coli monitoring

Appalachian Microbes

Under the mentorship of Dr. Rachel Washburn, I am conducting a literature review examining microbial communities across the Appalachian Mountains. My research explores how the region's diverse geology, topography, and environmental conditions may contribute to microbial diversity and seeks to identify major knowledge gaps in our understanding of Appalachian microbial ecosystems. This work aims to synthesize existing findings and establish future directions for microbial research in the region.

Tools: Literature review, cave systems, microbial ecology

Polluted Caves and Their Effects on Microbial Communities

Working under Dr. Rachel Washburn, I am leading a literature review investigating how anthropogenic and biological factors influence karst environments worldwide. My research examines the impacts of urbanization, agriculture, tourism, wildlife, and pollution on karst systems while highlighting the limited understanding of how microbial communities contribute to karst processes and ecosystem health. This project seeks to identify critical gaps in the literature.

Tools: Literature review, cave systems, microbial ecology
