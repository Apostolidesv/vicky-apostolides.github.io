About Me

I am an undergraduate Geology student at Appalachian State University, focused on hydrology, geohazards, and geospatial analysis.

Through coursework, research, internships, and field experiences, I have developed skills in data collection, geospatial analysis, and technical writing.

I am particularly interested in flooding and hope to apply my skills toward flood mitigation and stream restoration.
