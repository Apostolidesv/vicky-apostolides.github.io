About Me

I am a geology student at Appalachian State University with interests in geomorphology, hydrology, remote sensing, GIS, and unmanned aircraft systems (UAS). My research focuses on using geospatial technologies to understand environmental change and improve monitoring of natural systems.

I am currently involved in projects examining vegetation succession on Alaskan proglacial braidplains, stream monitoring using drone imagery, and cave microbiology research.

Research Interests

  • Remote Sensing and GIS
  • UAS / Drone Mapping
  • Hydrology and Water Resources
  • Geomorphology
  • Environmental Monitoring
  • Machine Learning for Geospatial Applications

Current Projects

Alaska Braidplain NDVI Analysis

Investigating vegetation succession on proglacial braidplains using Landsat imagery, NDVI analysis, and GIS techniques.

UAS Stream Monitoring

Using drone imagery and photogrammetry to evaluate stream conditions, riparian vegetation, and ecosystem health.

Cave Microbiology Research

Studying microbial communities and environmental conditions in cave systems through field sampling and laboratory analysis.
